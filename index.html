PROJECT 01 / INTERACTIVE GAME
❄️
Featured project

Icefall Challenge

A browser-based Himalayan climbing game. Players move a climber left and right to avoid falling ice balls, manage three lives, and choose between a safer route and a faster, riskier route. The goal is to reach 8,000 metres.

Building this project helped me explore how a game can combine clear instructions, interactive controls, changing difficulty, scoring, and meaningful choices.
